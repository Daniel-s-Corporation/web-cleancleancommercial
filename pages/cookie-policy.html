1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and tags. They help a website remember your preferences, understand how it is used, and measure advertising. In this policy, we refer to all of these as “cookies.”

First-party cookies are set by our Site. Third-party cookies are set by other companies, such as Google, whose services we use.

2. Our approach (US / Florida)

Clean Clean Commercial Cleaning serves businesses in Florida and our Site is intended for US visitors. US privacy laws, including the Florida Digital Bill of Rights, generally follow an opt-out model: optional cookies may be used once you have been clearly informed, and you must be able to opt out easily at any time.

Accordingly:

  • On your first visit, a cookie notice explains our use of cookies and offers equally visible Accept All and Reject Non-Essential buttons, plus a Customize option.
  • Until you make a choice, analytics and advertising cookies are enabled by default.
  • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we automatically treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising, and we turn off Marketing cookies.
  • Your choice is saved for 12 months, after which we will ask again. You can change it at any time from the Your Privacy Choices link in the footer.

We use Google Tag Manager with Google Consent Mode v2, which passes your choices to Google tags so they adjust their behavior (for example, not setting analytics or advertising cookies when you have opted out).

3. Cookie categories

Strictly necessary

Required for the Site to work securely and to remember your cookie choices. These cannot be turned off.

Analytics

Help us understand how many people visit the Site, how they find it, and which pages are useful, so we can improve it. We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager.

Marketing & advertising

Help us measure whether our ads lead to quote requests and, where enabled, show relevant ads on other websites. Under some US state laws, these may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information or “targeted advertising.”

5. Third-party content

  • Google Fonts: our fonts are delivered by Google. Google Fonts does not set cookies, but your browser sends your IP address to Google to download the fonts.
  • WhatsApp and Instagram: links to these services do not set cookies on our Site. If you click them, Meta's own cookies and privacy policies apply on their platforms.
  • Netlify: our hosting provider processes technical data (such as IP addresses) to deliver and secure the Site.

6. Managing your choices

Change your cookie preferences now:

  • Our cookie settings: use the Your Privacy Choices link in the footer of any page to accept, reject, or customize optional cookies.
  • Global Privacy Control: turn on GPC in a supporting browser or extension and we will honor it automatically. Learn more at globalprivacycontrol.org.
  • Browser settings: most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites work.
  • Google tools: you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on and manage ad personalization in My Ad Center.
  • Industry opt-outs: you can opt out of interest-based ads from participating companies at optout.aboutads.info.
