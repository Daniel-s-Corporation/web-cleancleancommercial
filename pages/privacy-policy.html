1. Who we are

Clean Clean Commercial Cleaning (“Clean Clean,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a family-owned commercial cleaning company based in Florida, serving businesses in Bradenton, Sarasota, Lakewood Ranch, Palmetto, and surrounding areas. This Privacy Policy applies to our website, cleancleancommercialcleaning.netlify.app (the “Site”), and to the information you share with us when you request a quote or contact us.

Our services are offered to businesses in the United States. This policy is written for US residents and is governed by US federal law and the laws of the State of Florida.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Quote request form: your full name, business name, phone number, email address, the service you need, and anything you write about your space (for example, square footage or cleaning schedule).
  • Direct communications: information you share when you call, text, email, or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, including your contact details and the content of your messages.
  • Service relationship: if you become a client, details needed to deliver and bill for our services, such as your business address, site access instructions, and billing contacts.

Information collected automatically

When you visit the Site, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Device and browser information (browser type, operating system, screen size, language).
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it (city or region level).
  • Usage information such as pages viewed, time on page, links clicked, the website that referred you, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Identifiers stored in cookies or similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data (such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, health information, or precise geolocation) through the Site. Please do not include this type of information in the quote form.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your quote requests and questions, and to prepare customized cleaning proposals.
  • To schedule, deliver, and invoice our cleaning services.
  • To communicate with you about your service, including scheduling updates and service-related messages.
  • To understand how visitors use the Site and improve its content and performance (analytics).
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising, where you have not opted out.
  • To protect the Site and our business, prevent spam and fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information only as described below:

  • Service providers who help us run our business and the Site, under contracts that limit their use of the data, including: Netlify (website hosting and form handling), Google (Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Google Fonts, and, if enabled, Google Ads), and Meta (WhatsApp and Instagram, when you choose to contact us through them).
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Clean Clean, our clients, or others.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business, subject to this policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

Some advertising and analytics cookies may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, or “targeted advertising,” under certain US state privacy laws. You can opt out at any time through , and we automatically honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal sent by your browser.

5. Cookies & tracking technologies

We use Google Tag Manager to manage tags on the Site, together with Google Consent Mode, so that analytics and advertising tags respect the choices you make in our cookie banner. In line with US practice, optional cookies are enabled by default (an “opt-out” model), and you can turn them off at any time.

For the full list of cookies, their purpose, and duration, please read our Cookie Policy. You can change your preferences at any time using the Your Privacy Choices link in the footer of every page.

Do Not Track: because there is no common industry standard for “Do Not Track” browser signals, the Site does not respond to them. We do, however, honor the Global Privacy Control signal as described above.

6. Your privacy rights

Florida residents

The Florida Digital Bill of Rights (Fla. Stat. §§ 501.701–501.722) gives Florida consumers rights over personal data held by businesses that meet the law's thresholds. Even where these thresholds may not apply to a business of our size, we voluntarily honor the following requests from Florida residents:

  • Access: confirm whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy of it.
  • Correction: correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion: delete personal data you provided or that we obtained about you.
  • Portability: receive your data in a portable, readily usable format.
  • Opt out: of the sale of personal data, targeted advertising, and profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Residents of other US states

Residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws (such as California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and others) may have similar rights to know, access, correct, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising. We will honor these requests as required by applicable law.

How to make a request

  • Email us at cleancleancommercialcleaning@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy Request,” or call 941-259-9604.
  • For cookie-based opt-outs, use or enable Global Privacy Control in your browser.

We will verify your request by matching the information you provide (for example, your name and the email or phone number you used with us) to our records. You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authorization. We will respond within 45 days, and may extend this once by an additional 45 days when reasonably necessary, in which case we will let you know.

Appeals: if we decline your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the subject line “Privacy Appeal.” We will respond in writing within 60 days. If you are not satisfied, you may contact the Florida Attorney General's Office or the attorney general of your state.

No discrimination: we will not deny services, charge different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised your privacy rights.

7. Calls, texts & WhatsApp

When you give us your phone number, we use it to respond to your request and to communicate about your service. We do not send marketing calls or text messages using automated systems without your prior express written consent, as required by the federal Telephone Consumer Protection Act and the Florida Telephone Solicitation Act. You can reply “STOP” to any text message or ask us at any time to stop contacting you. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Quote requests from people who do not become clients are generally deleted within 24 months. Client records are kept for the length of the business relationship and as long afterward as needed to meet tax, accounting, and legal requirements. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings (no longer than 14 months).

9. Security & breach notification

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Site and access controls on our business accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by the Florida Information Protection Act (Fla. Stat. § 501.171), generally no later than 30 days after we determine a breach has occurred, and will notify the Florida Department of Legal Affairs when required.

10. Children's privacy

Our Site and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and we do not sell the personal information of anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party links & services

The Site links to third-party services such as WhatsApp, Instagram, and Google. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review their policies before sharing information with them.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If the changes are material, we will provide a more prominent notice on the Site.
